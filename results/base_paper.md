# FoodLoop on the base paper's instances (Dubey & Tanksale, 2023)

Instances rebuilt from Cordeau et al. (2001) pr01-pr20 with the base paper's rules (see tools/base_paper.py for the rules and assumptions A1-A5). Objective = routing cost + 500 per vehicle hired + 1000 per unit of unmet pickup or delivery (base paper eq. 1). Base-paper values are copied from the thesis chapter (Tables 4.5 and 4.6); they were obtained on the authors' own random demand draws, so only the balanced sub-classes (1, 4, 7), where no penalty is unavoidable, are compared head to head.
